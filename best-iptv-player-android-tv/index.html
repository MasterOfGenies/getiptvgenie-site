Where Couchbird wins

It finds the game for you

Open the Sports tab and you see every NFL, NBA, NHL, MLB, college football, college basketball, WNBA and MLS game today, with live scores, start times and the networks carrying it. Couchbird matches those networks against your channel list, including regional sports networks and event channels, and outlines the games you can actually watch in green. One click starts the game. How the sports guide works →

Your local news, first

Playlists bury local stations among thousands of channels. Couchbird knows the ABC, CBS, NBC, FOX and CW stations for 43 US TV markets and lists yours first. On a VPN it asks for your home city instead of guessing from the wrong location.

Clean, and nothing hiding in it

No ads, no analytics, no crash-reporting services, no account with us. Your provider login is encrypted with your device's secure hardware and never leaves it. Read the privacy policy.

Built for the remote

Up and Down change channels instantly, Left jumps back to the last channel, OK opens the channel list over the video, and a see-through quick menu gives you pause, restart, audio, subtitles, picture size and a sleep timer without leaving the show. Back always returns you to the section you came from.

Record, follow your teams, move TVs in a minute

Press Record while you watch, or hold OK on any program in the guide to schedule it; recordings keep going with the app closed. Follow your teams once and get a “starting now” prompt when they play on your channels. Got a new TV? Copy your whole setup to it over Wi-Fi with an 8-digit code.

Where TiviMate wins

TiviMate is the long-time favorite for a reason. It has years of polish, its Premium plan can record to a network drive as well as the device, and it lets you customize almost every part of the guide's look. If you want to record to a home server or tweak every detail, TiviMate is still ahead there.

Where IPTV Smarters wins

IPTV Smarters runs on more kinds of devices, including iPhone and iPad, and has a free version. If you need one app across phones, tablets and TVs, it covers more ground. The free version shows ads; the paid Pro version removes them.

Switching from TiviMate or Smarters?

You sign in with the same Xtream Codes login or M3U link you use today. See Couchbird as a TiviMate alternative and Couchbird as an IPTV Smarters alternative.

Speed on real devices

Couchbird is written natively for Android TV, with no web views and nothing running in the background while you watch. It handles playlists of 50,000+ channels, and on lower-memory Fire TV Sticks it automatically shrinks image caches and video buffers to stay smooth. Best experience: NVIDIA Shield TV Pro. Questions

What is the best IPTV player for Android TV?

It depends on what you watch. For sports and local news, Couchbird is built to answer “where's the game?” and “where's my local news?” in one click. If you want to record to a home server or network drive, TiviMate is the better pick today. If you also need an iPhone or iPad app, IPTV Smarters covers more platforms.

Is Couchbird a good TiviMate alternative?

Yes, especially if you watch live TV, sports and local channels. Couchbird has a full TV guide, favorites, catch-up, multi-view and recording, plus live scores matched to your channels and alerts when your teams play. TiviMate can also record to a network drive and lets you customize the guide more.

Does Couchbird work with my IPTV provider?

If your provider gives you an Xtream Codes login or an M3U playlist link, yes. XMLTV guide links are supported too. Couchbird doesn't include any channels.

Does Couchbird have ads?

No. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking in any version, including the free trial.
