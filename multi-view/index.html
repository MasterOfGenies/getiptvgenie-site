Multi-view

IPTV multi-view: watch two or four channels at once

Every game on one screen. IPTV Genie tiles live channels edge to edge with almost no borders, and you choose whose sound you hear.

Two side by side

Split the screen between two channels on any Fire TV, Android TV or Google TV device.

Four at once

A 2×2 grid of live channels on NVIDIA Shield, Fire TV Cube and other devices with more memory.

Picture-in-picture

Keep the main game full screen with a second channel in a small window.

How to use it

  1. Start watching a channel, then open the quick menu (Right then Down, or hold OK).

  2. Choose Watch 2, Watch 4 or Picture-in-picture. From Sports, hold OK on a game to watch all the live games together.

  3. Use the arrows to pick a tile; its outline turns red and its sound plays. Channel up/down changes just that tile.

  4. Hold OK for layout options, and press Back to return to one channel.

Multi-view is part of the IPTV sports guide, which finds every game on your channels and outlines the ones you can watch.

Multi-view questions

How many channels can IPTV Genie show at once?

Two side by side on any supported device, or four at once on devices with more memory, like NVIDIA Shield and Fire TV Cube. There's also picture-in-picture: one full screen with a small window.

Which channel's sound do I hear?

The one you highlight. Move between tiles with the arrows and the sound follows.

Does multi-view use more of my provider's connections?

Yes, each picture is its own stream. Two-at-once needs a plan with at least two connections, four-at-once needs four.

Why can't my Fire TV Stick show four at once?

Sticks have 1–2 GB of memory, so IPTV Genie keeps them to two pictures to stay smooth. Four at once is offered on devices with more memory.
