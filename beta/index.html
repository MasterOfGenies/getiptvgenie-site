Welcome to the Couchbird beta.

Thanks for helping test Couchbird before launch. You get every feature free for 30 days, starting the first time you open the app. No account, no payment details, nothing to cancel.

Install on Fire TV or Fire Stick

  1. From the home screen, search for Downloader (the orange app by AFTVnews) and install it.
  2. Go to Settings → My Fire TV → Developer options → Install unknown apps and turn on Downloader. If you don't see Developer options, open Settings → My Fire TV → About and click your device name 7 times.
  3. Open Downloader, type couchbird.app/beta.apk and press Go.
  4. Choose Install, then Open.

Install on NVIDIA Shield, Google TV or Android TV

  1. Install Downloader by AFTVnews from the Google Play Store.
  2. Allow it to install apps. On a Shield: Settings → Device Preferences → Security & restrictions → Unknown sources, then turn on Downloader. On Google TV: Settings → System → About, click Android TV OS build 7 times, then Settings → Apps → Security & restrictions → Unknown sources.
  3. Open Downloader, type couchbird.app/beta.apk and press Go.
  4. Choose Install, then Open.

Getting started

Sign in with your IPTV provider's Xtream Codes login or M3U playlist link. Couchbird is a player only and doesn't include any channels. No provider handy? Press Try the demo to look around with sample channels.

The beta installs as Couchbird Beta, separate from the store version, so it won't get in the way when Couchbird launches.

What we'd love you to try

  • Sports: open Today's games and check that the games you care about light up and play.
  • The guide: scroll it fast. Is it smooth with your full channel list?
  • Everyday watching: channel surfing, My Guide, recording, multi-view, movies and shows.
  • Anything confusing. If you had to stop and think, we want to hear it.

Sending feedback

In the app, open Settings → Send a bug report. It includes the technical details we need and leaves out your provider's address and login. Or email support@couchbird.app with anything at all.

When the 30 days are up

The app tells you the beta has ended. You're never charged, and you can uninstall it any time. The beta counts its 30 days using the internet's date, so keep your TV's date and time set to automatic. This beta build stops working for everyone after February 20, 2027. Want to know the day Couchbird launches? Join the launch list.
