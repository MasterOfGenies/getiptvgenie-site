NVIDIA Shield setup guide

Install Couchbird on NVIDIA Shield, the best IPTV box there is

Coming soon to Google Play. These are the steps for launch day; get notified when it's out.

The Shield is the fastest Android TV box you can buy, and Couchbird uses all of it: instant channel changes, huge playlists, and four games at once in multi-view.

Couchbird is coming to the Google Play Store. Once it's listed:

  1. Find it

    Open the Google Play Store on your Shield and search Couchbird. You can also say “Couchbird” to the Google Assistant.

  2. Install it

    Select Install. Google Play keeps it updated automatically.

  3. Sign in

    Open Couchbird, pick Xtream Codes or M3U playlist, and enter your provider's details. Your 7-day free trial starts right away.

Google TV and Chromecast

Same steps on Google TV Streamer, Chromecast with Google TV and TVs with Google TV built in.

Get the most out of a Shield

  • Watch four at once. In the player, hold OK to open the quick menu and choose Watch 4. Shield runs four live channels side by side, edge to edge.
  • Try AI upscaling. On 2019 Shield models, AI-enhanced upscaling in the Display & Sound settings sharpens 720p and 1080p channels on a 4K TV.
  • Use Ethernet. The Shield's gigabit port is the single best fix for buffering.
  • Map a button. The Shield remote's customizable button can open Couchbird straight from anywhere.

Why Couchbird on a Shield

Most IPTV players were designed for slow sticks and feel the same on a Shield. Couchbird scales up: it matches live sports to your channels in the background, keeps big guides responsive, and runs multi-view at full quality. See how it compares to TiviMate and Smarters →

Shield questions

Is NVIDIA Shield good for IPTV?

It's the best Android TV box for IPTV. Its processor and memory handle huge playlists and four-at-once multi-view easily, and the Shield TV Pro has the most headroom.

Shield TV or Shield TV Pro for Couchbird?

Both are excellent. The Pro has more memory and storage, which helps with four-channel multi-view and very large playlists.

Does Couchbird work on Google TV and Chromecast?

Yes. The same steps work on Google TV Streamer, Chromecast with Google TV and TVs with Google TV built in.

Where do I get Couchbird for Shield?

From the Google Play Store, where it's coming soon. Search for Couchbird on your Shield and choose Install.
