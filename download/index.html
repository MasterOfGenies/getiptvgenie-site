Before you install

  • A supported TV device. Fire TV Stick (2nd gen or newer, Lite included), Fire TV Stick 4K and 4K Max, Fire TV Cube, NVIDIA Shield, Chromecast with Google TV, Google TV Streamer, or an Android TV running Android 7.1 or newer.
  • Your provider's details. Either an Xtream Codes login (server address, username and password) or an M3U playlist link. IPTV Genie is a player only: it doesn't include any channels.
  • A steady connection. Live TV streams constantly, so wired Ethernet or strong 5 GHz Wi-Fi works best. Most providers suggest around 10 Mbps for HD and 25 Mbps for 4K.
  • Space for recordings (optional). An hour of HD takes roughly 1.5 to 3 GB. Watching live needs no extra space.

Try it before you sign in

On the first screen, choose Try the demo. It loads a few sample channels, films, a TV guide and a demo scoreboard we made ourselves, so you can look around the guide, Sports, multi-view and recording before connecting a provider.

Updates

The Amazon Appstore and Google Play update IPTV Genie for you. Your sign-in, My Guide, favorites and recordings stay on your TV through every update.

Common questions

How much does it cost?

The first 7 days are free with every feature. After that it's $5.99 a year or $19.99 once for lifetime, bought through the store you installed from. See plans.

Do I need an account with you?

No. There's nothing to register. Your provider login stays encrypted on your TV.

Can I move my setup to another TV?

Yes. Install IPTV Genie on the new TV, choose Copy from another TV, and approve it on the old one. It travels straight between your TVs on your home network.

Not supported: Fire TV Stick 4K Select and Fire TV Stick HD (Vega OS), Roku, Apple TV, and Samsung or LG TVs' own systems. See the recommended devices.
