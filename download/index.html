Get IPTV Genie

Coming to Google Play and the Amazon Appstore.

Install it like any other app: no sideloading, no developer settings. Free for 7 days, no account needed. Sign in with your own IPTV provider.

Fire TV (Stick, Cube, Fire TV smart TVs)

  1. SearchOn the Fire TV home screen, go to Find → Search and type “IPTV Genie”.
  2. GetSelect IPTV Genie and choose Get. The Amazon Appstore installs it and keeps it updated.
  3. Open and sign inEnter your provider's Xtream Codes login or M3U playlist link. Full guide: Fire Stick setup.

NVIDIA Shield, Android TV and Google TV

  1. SearchOpen the Google Play Store on your TV and search “IPTV Genie”.
  2. InstallChoose Install. Google Play keeps it updated automatically.
  3. Open and sign inEnter your provider's details. Full guide: NVIDIA Shield setup.

Not supported: Fire TV Stick 4K Select and Fire TV Stick HD (Vega OS), Roku, Apple TV, and Samsung or LG TVs' own systems. See the recommended devices.
