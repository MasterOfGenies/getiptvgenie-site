IPTV sports guide

The IPTV player with live sports scores that finds the game for you

Finding the game in a long channel list is a chore. Couchbird shows you today's games, the live score, and which of your channels is carrying it. One click and you're watching.

Every game today, live

NFL, NBA, NHL, MLB, college football, men's college basketball, WNBA and MLS. Live scores, start times in your time zone, and the networks for every game.

Matched to your channels

Couchbird reads each game's networks and finds them in your playlist: national networks, regional sports networks and one-off event channels. Games you can watch get a teal outline.

“On your channels” filter

Skip the games you can't watch. One list of everything your provider actually carries tonight, sorted by start time.

Multi-view: 2 or 4 at once

Watch two games side by side, or four at once on a Shield or Fire TV Cube. Edge to edge, almost no borders, and you pick which one you hear.

Follow your teams

Hold OK on any game and follow either team. Every game of theirs on your channels gets a “starting now” prompt automatically, and Sports gets a ★ My teams list.

Record the game

Press Record and it stops by itself when the game ends, or add 15 minutes, 30 minutes or an hour for overtime. It keeps going if you close the app.

How sports matching works

  1. Couchbird gets today's schedule with each game's TV networks. Only league names are requested; nothing about you is sent.

  2. It indexes your channels in the background, understanding names like “US: ESPN 2 HD”, “NBC Sports Bay Area” or “NBA 05: Lakers vs Celtics”, regions and quality tags included.

  3. It matches networks and team names to your channels and your guide, so even event channels are found.

  4. You press OK. The game starts on your channel.

Built for game night

  • Instant channel surfing: Up and Down change channels; holding the button skips without loading each one.
  • Last channel: Left jumps between two games instantly.
  • Type a channel number on the remote to go straight to it.
  • Restart and catch-up: missed the kickoff? If your provider offers catch-up, restart the game from the beginning. Pause live TV, too.
  • Sleep timer for late West Coast games.

Team and league names belong to their owners. Couchbird shows scores and schedules; it doesn't provide any streams. What you can watch depends on your IPTV provider.

Sports questions

Which leagues does the IPTV sports guide cover?

NFL, NBA, NHL, MLB, college football, men's college basketball, WNBA and MLS, with live scores and the TV networks for every game.

How does Couchbird know which games I can watch?

It reads each game's TV networks and matches them to your channel list and guide, including regional sports networks and one-off event channels. A teal outline means the game is on your channels.

Can I watch two or four games at once?

Yes. Multi-view plays two or four live channels at once with almost no borders. Four-at-once needs a stronger device like an NVIDIA Shield or Fire TV Cube; sticks run two.

Does Couchbird add any channels?

No. It only organizes and plays the channels in your own subscription. It never adds, unlocks or recommends channels or streams.

Can it remind me when a game starts?

Yes. Set a reminder on any game or program, or follow your teams so their games get reminders automatically. Couchbird offers to switch when it starts, even mid-movie.

Can I record a game?

Yes. Press Record in the player or hold OK on the game in the guide to schedule it. Recordings save to your TV's storage and use one provider connection while they run.
