Recording

IPTV player with recording for Fire Stick, Android TV and Shield

An IPTV player with a built-in recorder. Hit Record while you watch, or schedule anything from the guide, and it's waiting on Home when you get back.

Two ways to record

  1. Record what's on

    While watching, open the quick menu (press Right then Down, or hold OK) and choose ⏺ Record. It records the program you're on and stops by itself when it ends. Watching a game? Add 15 minutes, 30 minutes or an hour for overtime.

  2. Schedule from the guide

    In the Guide, highlight any upcoming program, hold OK, and choose Record. Couchbird starts a minute early and runs two minutes long, so you don't lose the opening or the finish when broadcasts run late.

Built to finish the job

  • Keeps going with the app closed. Recording runs in the background and keeps the TV's connection awake, so you can switch to other apps while it records.
  • Survives restarts. Scheduled recordings come back after the TV reboots or the app updates; anything the TV missed is clearly marked.
  • Protects your storage. It won't start when space is low and stops before the TV fills up.
  • Plays like a movie. Your recordings appear in a Recordings row on Home, with pause, rewind and fast-forward.
  • Private. Recordings stay on your TV. The saved schedule is encrypted on the device because stream links contain your login.

Good to know

A recording uses one of your provider's connections while it runs. On newer Google TV devices (Android 12 and later) Couchbird asks once for permission to start recordings on time. Record only what you're authorized to watch, for your own viewing. Terms

Recording questions

Can Couchbird record live TV?

Yes. Press Record in the player to record what's on, or hold OK on any program in the guide to schedule it. Recordings are saved to your TV's own storage and play from Home.

Does it keep recording if I close the app?

Yes. Recording runs in the background with a small notification, so you can close Couchbird or watch other apps. Scheduled recordings start on their own, even after the TV restarts.

How much space does a recording take?

It depends on the channel's quality: roughly 1–3 GB per hour for HD. Couchbird won't start a recording below about 800 MB free and stops one before the TV runs out of space. Settings → Recordings shows how much is free.

Can I watch another channel while recording?

If your provider allows two or more connections, yes. A recording uses one connection, so on a one-connection plan you can't watch something else at the same time; Couchbird warns you when that's the case.

What if a game runs over?

When you press Record, choose how long to keep going after the guide's end time: right when it ends, or 15 minutes, 30 minutes or 1 hour extra. For games, 1 hour extra is listed first. Every recording also runs 2 minutes long by default.

Can I record to a USB drive or network storage?

Not yet. Recordings go to the TV's internal storage.
