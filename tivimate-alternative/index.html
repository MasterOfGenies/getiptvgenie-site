TiviMate alternative

A TiviMate alternative built for sports and local channels

TiviMate is a great IPTV player, and a lot of people never leave it. Couchbird is for the ones who mostly watch live TV and want to find the game or their local news without scrolling through thousands of channels.

What you get that TiviMate doesn't build in

  • Live scores matched to your channels. The Sports tab lists today's NFL, NBA, NHL, MLB, college, WNBA and MLS games and outlines the ones on your channels in green. How it works
  • Your local stations first. ABC, CBS, NBC, FOX and CW for 43 US TV markets, listed first, and VPN-aware. Local channels
  • Alerts when your teams play on your channels, with a “starting now” prompt.
  • Copy your setup to another TV over Wi-Fi with an 8-digit code, login included if you choose.
  • No ads, no analytics, no trackers in any version, including the free trial.

What works the same way

Both play your provider's Xtream Codes or M3U playlist with an XMLTV guide on Fire TV, Android TV, Google TV and NVIDIA Shield. Both have favorites, catch-up when your provider offers it, multi-view and recording. In TiviMate, multi-view and recording are part of the Premium plan.

Where TiviMate is still ahead

TiviMate's Premium plan can record to a network drive as well as the device, and it lets you customize almost every part of the guide. It also has years of polish. If those matter most to you, stay with TiviMate.

Switching takes a few minutes

  1. Have your provider's details ready: the Xtream Codes server, username and password, or your M3U link.

  2. Install Couchbird from Google Play or the Amazon Appstore (coming soon).

  3. Sign in and your channels, guide, movies and shows load. Your 7-day free trial starts.

  4. Add favorites by holding OK on a channel, and set your home city in Settings → Home location.

TiviMate alternative questions

Is Couchbird a good TiviMate alternative?

For live TV, sports and local channels, yes. It has a full TV guide, favorites, catch-up, multi-view and recording, plus live scores matched to your channels and alerts when your teams play. If you record to a network drive or customize every part of the guide, TiviMate is still the better fit.

Can I use my TiviMate login in Couchbird?

You use the same details from your IPTV provider: the Xtream Codes server, username and password, or the M3U playlist link. Couchbird doesn't need anything from TiviMate itself.

Do my TiviMate favorites carry over?

No. Favorites are set up again in Couchbird: hold OK on a channel to add it to My Guide.

Can I keep TiviMate installed while I try Couchbird?

Yes. The two apps don't affect each other. Keep in mind that each app playing at the same time uses one of your provider's connections.
