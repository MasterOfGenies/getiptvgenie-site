Couchbird privacy policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026 · couchbird.app

Short version: The Couchbird app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it sends no personal data to us. If you buy on our website or email us, we receive only what's needed to deliver your license and help you.

What stays on your device

Your provider login (server, username, password, playlist and guide links) is stored only on your device, encrypted with a key held by your device's secure hardware (Android Keystore). The same applies to your favorites, My Guide, watch history, playback positions, reminders, language and area settings, and the optional Adult PIN, which is stored only as a salted one-way hash. Cloud backup of the app's data is turned off, so none of this is copied to Google or Amazon backups. Uninstalling the app deletes it all.

Who the app talks to, and why

  • Your IPTV provider, at the address you enter, to sign in and load your channels, guide and streams. This happens under your provider's own privacy terms.
  • A sports scoreboard service, to show today's games and the networks carrying them. Only the league names are requested; nothing about you is sent.
  • ipinfo.io, once, to work out your approximate TV market (city level) for the Local channels list. As with any web request, that service sees your internet address. It never runs while a VPN is on. You can set your home location by hand or turn this off in Settings → Home location.
  • Image hosts named in your provider's catalog and the scoreboard (channel logos, posters, team logos), to show pictures.
  • Google Play or the Amazon Appstore, only when you start a purchase or restore one. Payments are handled entirely by them; we never see your card details.

Copying your setup and recordings

“Send my setup to another TV” sends your settings, and your login only if you choose, directly from one of your TVs to another on your home network. It's encrypted with a key made from the 8-digit code, you approve it on the sending TV, and it never goes through us or any server. Recordings are saved only on your TV's own storage.

Purchases and support

Couchbird is sold through Google Play and the Amazon Appstore, which handle payment under their own privacy policies; we never see your card details or your store account. If you email support@couchbird.app, we receive your message and email address and use them only to answer you. Settings → Send a bug report first shows you everything it contains: app version, TV model, memory, catalog counts, recent playback or recording problems and the last crash, with your provider's address, username and password removed. It is sent only if you choose Send, from your own email app. To see or delete what we have, email us.

This website

couchbird.app uses no cookies, analytics or tracking scripts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages and served through Cloudflare, which, like any web host, receive your internet address when you load a page.

The launch waitlist

If you join the waitlist, the only thing collected is the email address you type in. It is forwarded to our support inbox (through Cloudflare's email service) and used for one thing: to tell you when Couchbird launches. It isn't stored on the website, shared, sold, or added to any marketing tool. To have it deleted, email support@couchbird.app.

What we don't do

No advertising or analytics libraries, no crash-reporting services, no selling or sharing of data, no accounts with us. The app supplies no content of its own.

Children

Couchbird is not directed at children under 13. The app collects no personal information from anyone; the website only receives an email address if someone chooses to join the waitlist.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: support@couchbird.app.
