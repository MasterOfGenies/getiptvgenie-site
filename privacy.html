Privacy policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026 · getiptvgenie.com

Short version: IPTV Genie has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We don't collect your personal data, and we have no servers that receive it.

What stays on your device

Your provider login (server, username, password, playlist and guide links) is stored only on your device, encrypted with a key held by your device's secure hardware (Android Keystore). The same applies to your favorites, My Guide, watch history, playback positions, reminders, language and area settings, and the optional Adult PIN, which is stored only as a salted one-way hash. Cloud backup of the app's data is turned off, so none of this is copied to Google or Amazon backups. Uninstalling the app deletes it all.

Who the app talks to, and why

  • Your IPTV provider, at the address you enter, to sign in and load your channels, guide and streams. This happens under your provider's own privacy terms.
  • A sports scoreboard service, to show today's games and the networks carrying them. Only the league names are requested; nothing about you is sent.
  • ipinfo.io, once, to work out your approximate TV market (city level) for the Local channels list. As with any web request, that service sees your internet address. It never runs while a VPN is on. You can set your home location by hand or turn this off in Settings → Home location.
  • Image hosts named in your provider's catalog and the scoreboard (channel logos, posters, team logos), to show pictures.
  • Google Play or the Amazon Appstore, only when you start a purchase or restore one. Payments are handled entirely by them; we never see your card details.

What we don't do

No advertising or analytics libraries, no crash-reporting services, no selling or sharing of data, no accounts with us. The app supplies no content of its own.

Children

IPTV Genie is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: support@getiptvgenie.com.
