IPTV Genie questions

Does IPTV Genie come with channels?

No. IPTV Genie is a player. You sign in with an Xtream Codes account or an M3U playlist from your own provider. We don't sell, include or recommend any streams.

Which devices does it run on?

NVIDIA Shield, Android TV and Google TV devices, and every Fire TV running Fire OS: Fire TV Stick, Lite, 4K, 4K Max, Cube and Fire TV smart TVs. The two newest Amazon sticks run Vega OS, which doesn't run Android apps yet.

How does it know which games I can watch?

It reads tonight's schedule and each game's TV networks, then matches them to your channel list and TV guide. A green outline means it found the game on your channels.

I use a VPN. Will local channels still work?

Yes. Set your home city in Settings → Home location. With a VPN on, IPTV Genie asks instead of guessing.

Is IPTV Genie a TiviMate alternative?

Yes, for live TV, sports and local channels. It has a full TV guide, favorites, catch-up and multi-view, plus live scores matched to your channels. It doesn't record to storage. See the full comparison.

Can I record shows?

Yes. Press Record in the player, or hold OK on a program in the guide to schedule it. Recordings save to your TV's storage and play from Home. A recording uses one of your provider's connections while it runs.

I have IPTV Genie on another TV. Do I have to set it up again?

No. On the old TV choose Settings → Send my setup to another TV, then on the new one choose Get setup from another TV and enter the 8-digit code. It copies over your Wi-Fi, encrypted. Your plan comes along through your Google Play or Amazon account.

Does IPTV Genie have ads?

No. No ads, no analytics and no trackers in any version, including the free trial.

Can I watch more than one channel at once?

Yes. Multi-view plays two channels side by side, or four at once on stronger devices like NVIDIA Shield and Fire TV Cube. Sound follows the screen you highlight.

What about my privacy?

There are no ads, analytics or accounts. Your login stays encrypted on your device. Read the privacy policy.

Where do I buy it?

Inside the app, through Google Play (Android TV, Google TV, NVIDIA Shield) or the Amazon Appstore (Fire TV). Try it free for 7 days first. Your plan follows your Google or Amazon account to your other TVs.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds go through Google Play or the Amazon Appstore under their refund rules. If something isn't working, email support@getiptvgenie.com first and we'll help.
