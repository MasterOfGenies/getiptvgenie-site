Questions

Does IPTV Genie come with channels?

No. IPTV Genie is a player. You sign in with an Xtream Codes account or an M3U playlist from your own provider. We don't sell, include or recommend any streams.

Which devices does it run on?

NVIDIA Shield, Android TV and Google TV devices, and every Fire TV running Fire OS: Fire TV Stick, Lite, 4K, 4K Max, Cube and Fire TV smart TVs. The two newest Amazon sticks run Vega OS, which doesn't run Android apps yet.

How does it know which games I can watch?

It reads tonight's schedule and each game's TV networks, then matches them to your channel list and TV guide. A green outline means it found the game on your channels.

I use a VPN. Will local channels still work?

Yes. Set your home city in Settings → Home location. With a VPN on, IPTV Genie asks instead of guessing.

What about my privacy?

There are no ads, analytics or accounts. Your login stays encrypted on your device. Read the privacy policy.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds go through the store you bought from (Google Play or Amazon), under their refund rules.
