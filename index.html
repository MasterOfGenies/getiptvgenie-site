Couchbird questions

Does Couchbird come with channels?

No. Couchbird is a player. You sign in with an Xtream Codes account or an M3U playlist from your own provider. We don't sell, include or recommend any streams.

Which devices does it run on?

NVIDIA Shield, Android TV and Google TV devices, and every Fire TV running Fire OS: Fire TV Stick, Lite, 4K, 4K Max, Cube and Fire TV smart TVs. The two newest Amazon sticks run Vega OS, which doesn't run Android apps yet.

How does it know which games I can watch?

It reads tonight's schedule and each game's TV networks, then matches them to your channel list and TV guide. A teal outline means it found the game on your channels.

I use a VPN. Will local channels still work?

Yes. Set your home city in Settings → Home location. With a VPN on, Couchbird asks instead of guessing.

Is Couchbird a TiviMate alternative?

Yes, for live TV, sports and local channels. It has a full TV guide, favorites, catch-up, multi-view and recording to your TV's storage, plus live scores matched to your channels. TiviMate can also record to a network drive. See Couchbird as a TiviMate alternative or the full comparison.

Can I record shows?

Yes. Press Record in the player, or hold OK on a program in the guide to schedule it. Recordings save to your TV's storage and play from Home. A recording uses one of your provider's connections while it runs.

I have Couchbird on another TV. Do I have to set it up again?

No. On the old TV choose Settings → Send my setup to another TV, then on the new one choose Get setup from another TV and enter the 8-digit code. It copies over your Wi-Fi, encrypted. Your plan comes along through your Google Play or Amazon account.

Does Couchbird have ads?

No. No ads, no analytics and no trackers in any version, including the free trial.

Can I watch more than one channel at once?

Yes. Multi-view plays two channels side by side, or four at once on stronger devices like NVIDIA Shield and Fire TV Cube. Sound follows the screen you highlight.

What about my privacy?

There are no ads, analytics or accounts. Your login stays encrypted on your device. Read the privacy policy.

Where do I buy it?

It isn't available yet: Couchbird is coming soon to Google Play and the Amazon Appstore. Once it's out, you buy it inside the app, through Google Play (Android TV, Google TV, NVIDIA Shield) or the Amazon Appstore (Fire TV). Try it free for 7 days first. Your plan follows your Google or Amazon account to your other TVs.

What's the best IPTV player for a Fire Stick?

The one that's fast on a stick's small memory and doesn't make you scroll. Couchbird is built for that: it opens on your own channels, finds tonight's games for you, and plays two channels at once even on a Fire TV Stick. TiviMate and IPTV Smarters are popular too; our honest comparison shows where each one is stronger.

How much space do recordings take?

An hour of HD is roughly 1.5 to 3 GB, saved to your TV's own storage. Settings shows how much room is left. A recording uses one of your provider's connections while it runs.

What do I need for multi-view?

Two channels at once works on any supported device. Four at once needs a stronger device such as NVIDIA Shield or Fire TV Cube, plus a plan from your provider that allows that many connections.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds go through Google Play or the Amazon Appstore under their refund rules. If something isn't working, email support@couchbird.app first and we'll help.
