Local channels

The IPTV player that puts your local channels first

Big playlists bury local stations among thousands of channels. IPTV Genie finds your city's ABC, CBS, NBC, FOX and CW stations and puts them first, with news at the top.

What it does

  • A Local list for your city in Channels, with your local stations grouped together and news first.
  • Local rises to the top of any channel list they're in.
  • 43 US TV markets covered, from New York and Los Angeles to Dallas–Fort Worth and beyond.
  • Works with VPNs. Your location is a setting, not a guess.

Set your home city

  1. Open Settings → Home location.

  2. Choose your city, or let IPTV Genie detect it (it never detects while a VPN is on).

  3. Open Channels → Local. Your stations are there, news first.

Location detection asks ipinfo.io once for your approximate market (city level). Details are in the privacy policy.

Local channel questions

How does IPTV Genie know my local channels?

It knows the ABC, CBS, NBC, FOX and CW stations for 43 US TV markets. It works out your market from your connection once, or you pick your city in Settings → Home location.

I use a VPN. Will I get the wrong city?

No. With a VPN on, IPTV Genie doesn't guess from the VPN's location; it asks you to pick your home city instead.

Can I see another city's local news?

Yes. Set Home location to any supported city, for example where you grew up.

Does this add local channels to my plan?

No. IPTV Genie only finds and organizes the local channels your provider already includes.
