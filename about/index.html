About IPTV Genie

IPTV Genie is an independent IPTV player for Fire TV, Android TV, Google TV and NVIDIA Shield, built by a small independent developer.

Why it exists

Most IPTV players treat a 20,000-channel playlist like a spreadsheet. Finding tonight's game, your local news or the show you were halfway through takes scrolling, guessing and patience. IPTV Genie was built to answer those questions in one or two clicks: what games can I watch tonight, where's my local news, and where did I leave off.

What we stand for

  • Your data stays yours. No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no trackers. Your provider login is encrypted on your device and never sent to us.
  • A player, not a provider. IPTV Genie doesn't sell, include or recommend any channels or streams. Use it with services you're authorized to watch.
  • Honest about what it does. Our comparison says where other players are better, too.
  • Tested before it ships. Every release runs hundreds of automated checks on a real NVIDIA Shield and on a low-memory test device set up like a Fire TV Stick.
