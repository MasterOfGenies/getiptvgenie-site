IPTV Smarters alternative

An IPTV Smarters alternative with no ads, built for the TV

IPTV Smarters runs on almost everything, phones included. Couchbird does one thing: it makes live TV on your Fire TV, Android TV or Shield fast to get around with a remote, with no ads in any version.

Why people switch

  • No ads, ever. The free version of IPTV Smarters shows ads; Couchbird has none, plus no analytics and no trackers.
  • Made for the remote. Up and Down change channels instantly, Left jumps back to the last channel, and OK opens the channel list over the video.
  • Finds the game for you. Live scores for today's games, matched to the channels in your playlist. Sports guide
  • Local news first. Your city's ABC, CBS, NBC, FOX and CW stations at the top. Local channels
  • Recording and multi-view built in: record now or from the guide, and watch two or four channels at once. Recording · Multi-view

Where IPTV Smarters is still ahead

It runs on more kinds of devices, including iPhone and iPad, and it has a free version. If you want one app across phones, tablets and TVs, IPTV Smarters covers more ground.

Switching takes a few minutes

  1. Have your provider's details ready: the Xtream Codes server, username and password, or your M3U link.

  2. Install Couchbird from Google Play or the Amazon Appstore (coming soon). Setup guides: Fire Stick, NVIDIA Shield.

  3. Sign in and your channels, guide, movies and shows load. Your 7-day free trial starts.

IPTV Smarters alternative questions

Is Couchbird a good IPTV Smarters alternative?

On Fire TV, Android TV, Google TV and NVIDIA Shield, yes. It's built only for TVs and the remote, with no ads in any version, a sports guide matched to your channels and local stations first. If you also need an app for iPhone or iPad, IPTV Smarters covers more devices.

Does Couchbird have ads?

No. There are no ads, no analytics and no trackers in any version, including the free trial.

Can I use my IPTV Smarters login in Couchbird?

You use the same details from your IPTV provider: the Xtream Codes server, username and password, or the M3U playlist link.

Is there an Couchbird app for iPhone?

Not yet. Couchbird is for Fire TV, Android TV, Google TV and NVIDIA Shield.
