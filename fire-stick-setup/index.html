Fire TV setup guide

Install IPTV Genie on a Fire TV Stick in 2 minutes

The best IPTV player for Fire Stick shouldn't take an hour to set up. Four steps, no computer needed, works on every Fire TV running Fire OS.

  1. Install Downloader

    On the Fire TV home screen, go to Find → Search, type Downloader, and install the orange app by AFTVnews. It's free.

  2. Allow installs from Downloader

    Go to Settings → My Fire TV → Developer options → Install unknown apps and turn Downloader on.

    No “Developer options”? Go to Settings → My Fire TV → About, select your device name 7 times until it says you're a developer, then go back.

  3. Get IPTV Genie

    Open Downloader and type the IPTV Genie code shown on our download page, then select Go. Choose Install when it finishes downloading.

  4. Open it and sign in

    Choose Open. Pick Xtream Codes or M3U playlist and enter the details from your IPTV provider. Your channels, guide, movies and shows load in seconds, and your 7-day free trial starts.

Optional: free up space

Back in Downloader, delete the downloaded file when it asks. IPTV Genie keeps working.

Put IPTV Genie on your home screen

Press the Home button and hold it, choose Apps, highlight IPTV Genie, press the Menu (☰) button and choose Move to front.

Fire Stick tips for smooth IPTV

  • Use 5 GHz Wi-Fi or Ethernet. Amazon's Ethernet adapter fixes most buffering on busy networks.
  • Add favorites. In the channel list, hold OK on a channel to add it to My Guide, so the channels you watch are always one click away.
  • Set your home city in Settings → Home location if you use a VPN, so your local news shows up first.
  • Restart the stick weekly (Settings → My Fire TV → Restart) to clear memory.

Fire Stick questions

Which Fire TV devices does IPTV Genie run on?

Every Fire TV running Fire OS: Fire TV Stick (2nd and 3rd gen), Fire TV Stick Lite, Fire TV Stick 4K, Fire TV Stick 4K Max, Fire TV Cube, and smart TVs with Fire TV built in. The Fire TV Stick 4K Select and the newest Fire TV Stick HD run Vega OS, which can't run Android apps, so they aren't supported.

Is installing with Downloader safe?

Downloader only fetches the file from getiptvgenie.com. Android checks the app's signature, and IPTV Genie's own updates are verified against a fingerprint before they install.

Will it be in the Amazon Appstore?

Yes, IPTV Genie is coming to the Amazon Appstore. Until then, install it with Downloader in about two minutes.

How do I update IPTV Genie on a Fire Stick?

The app checks for updates once a day and asks before installing. You can switch to automatic or turn checks off in Settings.

Why is my Fire Stick slow with a big playlist?

Older sticks have 1 GB of memory. IPTV Genie adapts automatically by using smaller image caches and video buffers. A wired connection and a weekly restart help most.
