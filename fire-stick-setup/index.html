Fire TV setup guide

How to install Couchbird on a Fire TV Stick

Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore. These are the steps for launch day; get notified when it's out.

Setting up an IPTV player on a Fire Stick shouldn't take an hour. Couchbird is coming to the Amazon Appstore: three steps, no sideloading, no computer, on every Fire TV running Fire OS.

  1. Find it

    On the Fire TV home screen, go to Find → Search and type Couchbird.

  2. Get it

    Select Couchbird and choose Get (or Download). The Amazon Appstore installs it and keeps it updated.

  3. Open it and sign in

    Choose Open. Pick Xtream Codes or M3U playlist and enter the details from your IPTV provider. Your channels, guide, movies and shows load in seconds, and your 7-day free trial starts.

Put Couchbird on your home screen

Press the Home button and hold it, choose Apps, highlight Couchbird, press the Menu (☰) button and choose Move to front.

Fire Stick tips for smooth IPTV

  • Use 5 GHz Wi-Fi or Ethernet. Amazon's Ethernet adapter fixes most buffering on busy networks.
  • Add favorites. In the channel list, hold OK on a channel to add it to My Guide, so the channels you watch are always one click away.
  • Set your home city in Settings → Home location if you use a VPN, so your local news shows up first.
  • Restart the stick weekly (Settings → My Fire TV → Restart) to clear memory.

Fire Stick questions

Which Fire TV devices does Couchbird run on?

Every Fire TV running Fire OS: Fire TV Stick (2nd and 3rd gen), Fire TV Stick Lite, Fire TV Stick 4K, Fire TV Stick 4K Max, Fire TV Cube, and smart TVs with Fire TV built in. The Fire TV Stick 4K Select and the newest Fire TV Stick HD run Vega OS, which can't run Android apps, so they aren't supported.

Where do I get Couchbird for Fire TV?

From the Amazon Appstore, where it's coming soon. Search for Couchbird on your Fire TV and choose Get. No sideloading and no developer options needed.

How do I update Couchbird on a Fire Stick?

The Amazon Appstore updates it automatically, like any other app.

Why is my Fire Stick slow with a big playlist?

Older sticks have 1 GB of memory. Couchbird adapts automatically by using smaller image caches and video buffers. A wired connection and a weekly restart help most.
